Business trade-off

Higher recall and stronger ranking performance, at the cost of lower precision. Recall rises 7.72 pp (about 8 more Private customers found per 100 actual); the share of flagged customers who are Mass rises from 27.16% to 30.51%. RM review helps manage this before service allocation.

Pilot loop
Predict→Segment→Allocate→Measure→Re-score

Business Head sets list size and assigns customers after RMs confirm capacity; Campaign Lead plans audience, message and timing; both review response, conversion, cost and RM feedback to continue, adjust or expand the pilot.

Read with care

Moderate discriminative power (test ROC-AUC 0.7521). Heavy reliance on external score and large-transaction amount, so external-data drift could reduce performance. Real conversion impact can only be confirmed through the pilot.
